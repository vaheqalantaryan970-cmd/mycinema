1. What is Cinema? 👤

Cinema is one of the most popular forms of entertainment in the world. It is the art of telling stories through moving images and sound. People watch films for entertainment, but movies can also teach us about history, culture, and different people.

Cinema has changed a lot over the years. The first films were very short and had no sound or color. Today, we have modern cinemas, 3D films, and amazing special effects.

Section 02 - 04

History & Evolution 🎞️

1895

The First Cinema

The Lumière brothers (Auguste and Louis) showed their short silent films to the public in Paris, France. One famous clip showed workers leaving a factory.

Early 1900s

Silent Movies & Charlie Chaplin

First films were black-and-white without spoken dialogue. Charlie Chaplin became world-famous for his funny silent films and his character, the Little Tramp.

1920s - 1930s

Sound & Technicolor

Sound allowed films to have spoken dialogue and recorded music ("Talkies"). Technicolor brought bright realism to movies.

Modern Era

CGI, 3D & Streaming

High-tech visual graphics, IMAX, and streaming services transformed how movies are created and viewed globally.
